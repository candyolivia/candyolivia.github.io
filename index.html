Candy Olivia Mawalim

Senior Lecturer, JAIST, Japan

Hi, I'm Candy! I am a senior lecturer at School of Information Science, Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST). My primary research interests are speech signal processing, machine learning, and social signal processing (SSP). My Ph.D. thesis focuses on privacy preservation and secure speech communication. In addition to primary research, I also carried out a research project on personality traits and communication skills modeling with Prof. Shogo Okada. I obtained my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in Information Science, JAIST, advised by Prof. Masashi Unoki.

Research interests: Speech and language processing, acoustic phonetics, trustworthy AI, human-computer interaction.

News

NOV 9-12, 2026

I attended APSIPA ASC 2026 in Bangkok, marking my seventh year participating in this conference. Two of my co-advised students presented their papers this year. It was an especially meaningful event for me, as I was honored to be one of invited speakers for the Women in APSIPA session—a wonderful moment to reflect on my growth within this community. I also had a great time catching up with fellow alumni and colleagues from my former supervisor's lab in Thailand.

OCT 5-9, 2026

I recently attended the ACM ICMI 2026 conference in Naples, Italy, for the first time. The conference featured a wide range of fascinating, multidisciplinary research, and its close-knit community made it easy to engage with fellow researchers. It was a very productive trip, highlighted by meeting several online collaborators in person for the first time.

SEP 27 - OCT 1, 2026

Three of our papers were recently presented at Interspeech 2026—including one resulting from our collaboration with Education Testing Service (ETS) (Privacy vs. Performance: Assessing Communication Utility of Anonymized Voice Features), one based on our student's master's thesis (Adaptive Multimodal Expert Specialization by Meta-Learning for Spoken English Assessment), and one presented at the SPSC Symposium (RocknRiver: Multilingual Speaker Anonymization System for Voice Privacy 2026 Challenge). Although I was unfortunately unable to attend in person this time, I am proud to see our work shared with the community.
